A Shift in AI

January 30, 2026 — by Terel Lewis

Artificial Intelligence is undergoing a fundamental transformation that extends far beyond incremental improvements in model capabilities. We're witnessing a paradigm shift in how AI systems are designed, deployed, and integrated into our daily lives and business operations.

This shift represents more than just technological advancement—it's a reimagining of the relationship between humans and intelligent systems. As AI moves from being a tool we use to an agent that acts on our behalf, the implications for businesses, developers, and end-users are profound and far-reaching.
